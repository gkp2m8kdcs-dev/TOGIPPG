Built for engineers, students & researchers

Every formula, every threshold band, and every chart is built directly into the app — no server, no account, no tracking.

ƒ Compute

Plug in physical properties and get instant dimensionless values with logarithmic gauges and side-by-side comparison.

Σ Classify

Custom regime classifier with threshold bands — turbulent vs. laminar, slip vs. continuum, conduction vs. convection, and more.

◉ Visualize

Bar, line, radar, donut, and log gauges let you inspect one regime or compare many at a glance.

⏱ History

Every computation is saved locally. Browse, revisit, and export past results directly from your device.

⌬ Derive

Step-by-step algebraic derivations show how each dimensionless group is constructed from the underlying physics.

⌗ Off-line

The entire library — formulas, thresholds, materials — is bundled in the binary. No internet required.
