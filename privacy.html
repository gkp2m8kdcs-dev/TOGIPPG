Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

TOGIPPG respects your privacy. This policy describes what information the app handles. The short version: we don't collect anything.

1. Data Collection

TOGIPPG does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no third-party advertising networks integrated into the app.

2. On-Device Storage

All inputs, computed values, and history records you create are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. You can erase them at any time via About → Settings → Clear History.

3. Network Access

TOGIPPG does not require a network connection. The app is fully functional off-line. If you choose to enable iCloud or system-level backups, your local data may be included in those backups according to your iOS settings.

4. Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone, motion data, or any other personal resource. With your permission, the app may access the camera and photo library only to:

  • Let you pick a profile picture for the About page.
  • Save analytics snapshots or dimensionless-number chart images you choose to export.

5. Children

TOGIPPG is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

Updates to this policy will be reflected in app releases. Continued use of the app after an update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, please reach out through the Support page or the App Store listing page for this app.

8. Disclaimer

All calculations are provided for educational and engineering reference only. Verify critical results independently. No warranty is provided regarding accuracy or fitness for any particular purpose.
