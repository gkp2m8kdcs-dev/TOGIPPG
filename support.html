Support

We're here to help — TOGIPPG is a small, focused utility, so support is straightforward.

Quick help

The app shows numbers in scientific notation. Can I change the format?
Yes. Open About → Settings → Default Precision and choose a different precision (e.g. 3 sig figs, Scientific, Engineering). The new format applies to all subsequent displays.
How do I delete my computation history?
Open About → Settings → Clear History and confirm. This permanently removes all saved entries from your device. The operation cannot be undone.
I think a calculated value is wrong. What should I check?
Most errors come from unit mismatches (e.g. mixing mm with m) or missing material properties. Double-check that every input is in the base SI unit shown beneath each field. If the formula itself looks off, please report it via the contact methods below — include the dimensionless number, the formula, your inputs, and the expected vs. observed value.
Does the app work without internet?
Yes. TOGIPPG is fully off-line. All formulas, thresholds, charts, and UI assets are bundled in the binary. If you notice any network traffic, please report it — that would be a bug.
Why was I asked for photo-library access?
Only if you choose to set a custom profile picture on the About page or export a chart snapshot. The permission is not requested for any other purpose. See our Privacy Policy for the full list of permissions.
How do I reset the app to a clean state?
You can clear history from inside the app (About → Settings → Clear History), or remove and reinstall the app from your device to also clear all preferences.

Reporting a calculation issue

To investigate a numerical disagreement as quickly as possible, please send:

  • The dimensionless number (e.g. Reynolds · Re).
  • The exact inputs you entered (with units).
  • The value the app produced.
  • The value you expected, and the source (textbook, paper, hand calc).

We treat calculation issues as high priority. Once verified, fixes will be folded into the next release with a note in the change log.
